Try the complete loop

  1. Open the client app and sign in as the client.
  2. Edit and save the assessment.
  3. Start a local practice session. Enter reps and load (0 kg for bodyweight), then save a set.
  4. Press “Sync saved sets”.
  5. Open the trainer dashboard and sign in as the coach. Select the client and review the assessment and synced set.
  6. Change the workout title and publish a new preview revision. Refresh the client’s workout preview to see it.

To explore invitations and coach assignments, use the administrator account. Records persist in the local database; restart the demo to continue. Offline practice records stay in this browser profile.
